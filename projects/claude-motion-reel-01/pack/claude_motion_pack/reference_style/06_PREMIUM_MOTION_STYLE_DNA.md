## 1. REF-A: EDITORIAL-KINETIC + LAYERED DEMONSTRATION

**Observed visual properties**
- Near-black ground with subtle red ambient bloom, fine grid/noise, thin rules/progress marks; major messages in extra-bold high-contrast typography.
- Emphasized words use a solid contrasting highlight strip or striking weight/color—not decorative glow on every word.
- App/screenshot surfaces appear as floating browser/phone/mockup cards, usually with a restrained perspective/tilt, outlined edge and shadow; some UI panels resemble dark translucent cards.
- Presenter appears as composited cutout in occasional beats; scene remains typographically driven rather than full character-animation.
- Punchy editorial cuts and impact transitions interrupt otherwise legible still compositions. Readable headline placement remains the priority.

| Source interval | Visible action and lesson | What to borrow |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0–2.3s** | Oversized central word is presented first; supporting words and presenter composite appear through rapid staged entrances; then a graphic wipe interrupts the page. | Strong typographic hierarchy, timed word beats, multi-layer composition, 1 sharp visual reset instead of constant swipes. |
| **5.0–7.2s** | Tilted sheet changes to tall phone/mockup; word emphasis and subtitle build in steps. | UI/artifact entrance with change of apparent scale/depth and voice-cued overlays; object remains stable long enough to see. |
| **7.2–9.2s** | Selected row outline advances down a list, with corresponding emphasized spoken words. | Selection/cursor travel that *explains* content and controlled highlight progression. |
| **19–24s** | Headline, browser window, and categorized rows take turns as the point of attention. | Clean focus cycling; object scene and copy remain connected. |
| **40–44s** | Object/UI transforms into large highlighted metric/hero message through a quick editorial reset. | One dominant metric and controlled continuity across scene changes. |

**Motion signature:** high editorial tempo, purposeful short acceleration, brief quiet holds, cut/wipe, stage text around hero screenshot, selection cue follows narration, minimal fake depth instead of random floating panels.

**Do not mislabel:** REF-A is **not primarily a physically glassy/true-3D reference**. Its value lies in typography, staging, and rhythm. Do not force B-style metal sculptures into an A-style piece unless the story benefits.

## 2. REF-B: CINEMATIC-OBJECT-DEPTH (STRONGEST DEPTH REFERENCE)

**Observed visual properties**
- Alternating dark charcoal grid world and warm ivory tabletop/card world: meaningful contrasts between editorial “worlds” across cuts.
- Rich scene metaphors visualized with objects: lamp and desk, layered folder/papers, miniature road/cars, stage/marquee, gauges, dial, balance scales, hand-operated mode selector, phone/chat mockups, folders/boxes and miniature interface props.
- Convincing separation of foreground, midground and background through perspective size, floor/plane anchoring, cast/contact shadows, occlusion and spotlight pools.
- A consistent warm gold accent connects metallic surfaces, status chips, dial lights and interactive states. Specular edges and contained bloom suggest polished materials—neither full-screen neon nor permanent motion blur.
- Tiny labels exist inside illustrative props, but the actual narrated sentence appears independently as legible type in a clean area. That separation makes dense visual scenes work on mobile.

## 3. REF-C: CLEAN-3D-EXPLAINER + CHARACTER STAGING

**Observed visual properties**
- Dominant white/light grid with occasional dark/pale-blue background switching to segment products. Large headlines mix expressive editorial display and clean sans-serif typography.
- Small rotund mascots appear volumetric with discernible highlights, rounded shading, ground/soft cast shadow, and consistent shape; the characters are used as functional labels rather than dominating every frame.
- Three feature cards appear one-by-one and align into a stable grid, then individual product worlds get their own simple visual stage. Elevated white cards have thin edges, soft shadow and occasionally gentle frosted/light-blue surface treatment.
- Product UI panels, phone mockups, large title cards and subtitles follow the narration. Lower caption chips use consistent spacing and high contrast.

## 4. SYNTHESIS: A BETTER DEFAULT QUALITY BAR

Do **not** concatenate 3 styles into every scene. Select from the following table according to the new video's goal:

| New brief category | Primary DNA | Secondary DNA | Useful production cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explainer AI or software with character | C | B | Mascot presents dimensional UI props, soft card depth, targeted dramatic hero reveal. |
| Business/strategy persuasive explainer | B | A | Diorama/metaphor and premium physical lighting with headline impacts. |
| Fast technical tutorial / steps | A | C | Kinetic hierarchical labels, in-context UI selection, subtle elevation and shadow. |
| Premium product/service launch | B | C | Tactile product/visual metaphor and restrained clean information card. |
| Pure minimalist, serious, documentary | user reference overrides | none required | Depth can be subtle, not obligatory decorative 3D. |

**Default visual hierarchy for premium dimensional scenes:**
1. Background environmental plane (atmosphere/grid/texture; does not compete with foreground).
2. Midground supporting shapes/environment, plausible occlusion and camera relationship.
3. Hero element (object/UI/phone/character) with meaningful depth, lighting and contact shadow.
4. Essential on-screen copy in highest-readability layer, usually not distorted by heavy perspective.
5. Focus marker, selection indicator, transition matte, small SFX-driven glint where useful.

**Glass/material interpretation:** in these videos *layered translucent/frosted-looking UI, rounded reflective surfaces, and polished lighting cues* are visible in selected scenes. There is **no evidence** that every surface has physically accurate light refraction. User's desired “glasses/glass” aesthetic is a **new enhancement guideline**, not a proven universal technique used throughout these clips. Implement glass deliberately where UI/subject calls for it and verify the rendered pixels.

## 5. REUSABLE NARRATIVE-DRIVEN MOTION MECHANISMS

| Mechanism | Story meaning | Choreography | Visual depth cue | Candidate implementation* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Layered browser reveal | Show a working digital interface | frame rises from back, rotates a few degrees, settles front | nested planes, contact shadow | CSS 3D + GSAP |
| Glass information plate | Reveal data without hiding context | plate changes angle, subtle edge glint, copy becomes sharp | backdrop difference, rim/reflection | CSS blur/tint + selective GSAP |
| Physical object proof | Make invisible process tangible | object placed; lid/dial/hinge acts; meaning emerges | occlusion, contact shadow, perspective | SVG+CSS 3D or Three.js |
| Depth tracking camera | Travel between two concepts | foreground shifts faster than backdrop, camera eases to target | differential parallax and Z | GSAP scene transform or 3D camera |
| State-change dial | Demonstrate a choice or automation | anticipation, finger/selector turn, detent, target light turns on | bevel, indicator, specular rim | GSAP rotation or Three.js |
| Feature-card fan-out | Compare multiple product options | hero card splits into sequenced cards, each finds anchor | scaling by Z, overlapping shadows | DOM 2.5D GSAP |
| Text/callout tracking | Guide attention to evidence | select/toggle/underline at word/VO cue | fixed anchor linked to hero | GSAP + SVG line |
| Character-guided explanation | Present context | looks/points/gestures at actual element, returns to hold | shading + plausible secondary motion | actual rig/sprite/Lottie where available |

*Always verify the installed HyperFrames runtime adapter and exact API. These are method choices, not proven source methods.
