# Prompt-Motion techniques

A paraphrased catalogue of what the strongest prompts on prompt-motion.com ask for and why it works. The prompts
belong to their creators; the client wants the **techniques** applied to his own videos.

Catalogue: `skills/aipreneur-reels-studio/references/prompt_motion_catalogue.md`
(categories with counts, techniques with entry links, REEL / LAND / BOTH fit tags, "Prompt patterns that work",
"Short one-shot prompts", full index of every entry).
Top picks are also summarised in `references/technique_bank.md` → "From prompt-motion.com".

## When to use
- Before storyboarding any new reel, explainer, promo, launch film, logo sting or UI loop for the client.
- When the client says the motion is "itu-itu aja" (repetitive) and you need fresh transitions or hooks.
- When writing a brief for a sub-agent: copy the prompt *shape* (role, inputs with defaults, direction + banned list,
  beat structure, build steps, gotchas, start protocol), never the wording.

## How to use
1. **Classify the brief** into one catalogue category: product launch film, UI animation, explainer/diagram,
   charts/data, showreel, characters/3D, typography, logo reveal, social/vertical reel, other.
2. **Read that category's section** (and "Fit summary"). Prefer techniques tagged REEL for 1080×1920 AIpreneur reels,
   LAND for landscape/square launch films, BOTH for either.
3. **Pick 2–4 techniques** that serve the story — not more. Typical mix: one hook device, one transition/through-line
   device, one sound/VO or QA practice, optionally one end-card move.
4. **Adapt to the brand**. These always win on conflicts:
   - `skills/aipreneur-reels-studio/references/brand.md` (navy/white themes, #01ccf7 / #294b77 accents only, Poppins,
     per-word captions, logo placement)
   - `skills/aipreneur-reels-studio/references/client_rules.md` (ATM, mascot required, no invented results, no cut text,
     Dakocan VO, deliverables)
   - `skills/aipreneur-reels-studio/references/voxel_reel.md` for voxel reels, `REELS_PIPELINE.md` for any reel.
   - Logos: Simple Icons (CC0) or the client's own files, built as 3D objects; record in `production/asset_credits.json`.
   Recolour every technique into the brand palette (e.g. gold particles → cyan, lime flood → navy/cyan flood), keep
   the mascot as host/guide object, keep VO in Indonesian.
5. **Never copy** another creator's product, brand, characters, likeness, locked copy, music or exact timeline. No
   anime/celebrity/IP crossovers. Quote at most a short phrase when naming a trick.
6. **Credit** in the project's production notes (e.g. `production/notes.md` or the storyboard file):
   `technique: <short name> — inspired by https://prompt-motion.com/<entry>`.
7. **QA as usual** (`references/qa.md`): stills at every beat/scene change, frame-difference scan for one-frame pops,
   no cut text, then the full render.

## Matching existing skills
Some gallery entries already became skills — use them directly when the brief matches:
`/product-launch-film` (food-craving launch film), `/beat-promo-film` (UGC promo), `/landing-flythrough-3d`
(landing page 3D scroll), `/business-explainer-30s` (5-scene explainer). For AIpreneur reels the base format stays
`/voxel-reels`, `/aip-remake` or `/aipreneur-reels-studio`; this skill only adds techniques on top.

## One-shot prompts
"Make a dynamic 15-second … go all out" style prompts are idea probes only: use them to sketch 2–3 quick options for a
hook or transition, then rebuild the winner inside the brand system. Never deliver a one-shot result as-is, and never
use one for anything with claims, numbers, logos or the mascot.

## Output of this skill
A short "Techniques" block in the storyboard: 2–4 lines, each = technique, how it is adapted to the brand, credit link.
