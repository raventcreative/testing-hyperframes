## Lessons from batch 1 (the real renderer behaves differently from our snapshot tool — two videos broke only in the final render)
1. **Final QA must use the real HyperFrames runtime**, not only `snap.cjs`:
   `cd <your folder> && npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.186 snapshot . --at t1,t2,… --no-end --timeout 60000 -o <scratch dir>`
   (writes `frame-NN-at-Xs.png` + `contact-sheet.jpg`). Check EVERY scene and the end card with it before you report. Use `snap.cjs` for quick iteration only.
2. **Script order differs in the render**: `robot_perf.js` may run AFTER your `buildReady` promise starts. If a build step uses anything defined in `robot_perf.js` (e.g. `ROBOT_POS`), wait for it: `new Promise(r => { const k = () => (typeof window.X === "function" ? r(1) : setTimeout(k, 20)); k(); })`.
3. **Never let build-time state leak**: if you call `tl.seek()` during a build (measuring poses), or `gsap.set()` an element that a later tween changes, then pin the element's initial state ON THE TIMELINE at 0 (`tl.set(el, {autoAlpha: 1, …}, 0)`). In batch 1 a receipt that fades out at 15 s was invisible from 0 s in the render because of this.
4. Do not tween `scale` in `fromTo` from-vars after backward seeks — use `scaleX`/`scaleY` (keep the `XS()` helper from mp10_03_nota if you like).
5. Run `npx --yes hyperframes@0.8.186 validate .` (or `check .`) — the only acceptable errors are the `lipsync.js` 404s.

## Concepts (VO scripts: `VO_SCRIPTS.md` sections 11–15)
- **11 · Mitos vs Fakta Pembukuan** (`mp10_11_mitos`, port 8811): three myth cards in 3D. Each card shows "MITOS" (red-ish stamp on paper, slightly crooked), then physically flips (3D card flip) to a clean navy "FAKTA" side with the fact. Myth 1 toko kecil vs usaha gede (small shop vs big tower scale gag), myth 2 omzet-only (a receipt with only the income column, the biaya column blank → laba "?"), myth 3 "nanti akhir tahun" (a 12-month pile crashing vs 12 small monthly steps). Brand: the three FAKTA cards stack into a pembukuan + laporan laba rugi card.
- **12 · Uang Pribadi vs Uang Usaha** (`mp10_12_pisah`, port 8812): one 3D wallet/jar receives sales money (coins/notes "Ilustrasi data"), then money leaks out to life expenses (jajan, cicilan, kirim keluarga — icons, no brands), the "untung?" gauge drops to "?". Mixed coins swirl (two colours, business = navy/yellow, personal = grey). "Pisahin dulu catatannya": a divider slices the stream into two labelled lanes "Pemasukan usaha" / "Pengeluaran pribadi". Brand: the business lane flows into a traceable pembukuan ledger.
- **13 · Laris Tapi Saldo Tipis** (`mp10_13_cerita`, port 8813): a short story with a fictional seller "Rina" (use a simple stylised silhouette/avatar or her shop counter + phone — no real person, no photo; small "Ilustrasi cerita" label). Orders pile up (busy shop, notifications) → end of month the bank balance card is thin (number shrinks). Reveal: tiny cost tokens (ongkir, kemasan, iklan, potongan platform) that were invisible become visible like a UV-light / X-ray sweep over the month. Brand: the tokens line up into a laporan laba rugi that reads clearly.
- **14 · Siapin 4 Data Ini** (`mp10_14_data`, port 8814): a checklist video. A 3D folder/box opens; four data items fly in one per beat, each a tangible 3D object with a check tick: 1 catatan penjualan (order sheet), 2 catatan biaya incl. ongkir & iklan (receipts + ad card), 3 mutasi rekening usaha (bank statement, generic), 4 nota & bukti transfer. Progress bar 1/4 → 4/4. Brand: the full box pours into MasterPajak AI → pembukuan → "Estimasi pajak" card (illustrative value, labelled; no rate).
- **15 · Tanya Cepat ke Robot** (`mp10_15_tanya`, port 8815): FAQ format — the robot is the star. Three question bubbles arrive like chat messages (or audience questions on cards), the robot answers each with a gesture and a crisp answer card. Q1 kecil perlu pembukuan? → "Perlu — biar tahu untung beneran". Q2 harus ngerti akuntansi? → "Nggak harus" + MasterPajak AI tidies notes into an easy report. Q3 bingung tanya siapa? → asisten AI + tim ahli "sesuai layanan". Counter "1/3, 2/3, 3/3". Keep it lively (bubbles pop in depth, camera pushes on each answer).
