1. `production/vo_lines.txt` → `python3 /home/user/testing-hyperframes/tools/el_tts_ts.py production/vo_lines.txt assets/audio/vo.mp3`

   Listen-check: faster-whisper `small` (id) on the whole VO + `medium` on three doubtful clips → every word spoken (details in VO_SCRIPT.md).

it; the result is a RAPOR (report card). `K.reel` (our flat 9:16 draft reel of a fictional UMKM "Sambal", CONTOH), `K.viewer` (AI viewer:
navy head, eyes, sparkle badge; `real: true` = a real person disc), `K.scene(ID)` (boilerplate: every initial state pinned at 0 in a

pop, shared exit, and `done()` = end marker + backward-seek patch + `tl.render(0, true, true)` + registration). Icons: lucide-static
1.70.0 (ISC), generated by the scratch tool `mkicons.py` into `vendor/r18icons.js`. No Higgsfield / Claude / Instagram logos or UI.
