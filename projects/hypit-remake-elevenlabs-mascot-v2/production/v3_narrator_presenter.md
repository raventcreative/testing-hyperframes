## Honest limits
- Mouth = shader viseme overlay (no jaw bone/blendshapes in the source GLB); reads best front / ¾. Interior viseme timing is estimated (±~0.1 s).
- Hands are single bones (mitten hands): no finger poses; "stop", "point" and "present" are arm + wrist poses.
- "Hai" is a wave gesture; the VO has no "hai", so nothing new is said or written.
- Walking toward camera is staged with an orthographic camera (scale + floor line), not true perspective.
