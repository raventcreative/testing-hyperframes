# MasterPajak AI · 10-video series — build brief (videos 2–10)

Client: **MasterPajak AI** (bookkeeping + tax helper for online sellers, by Muamalah Pajak). Format: 1080×1920 IG Reels / TikTok, 30–36 s each, Indonesian.
Reference implementation (already approved style): `projects/mp10_01_berita/` — read its `index.html`, `robot_perf.js`, `tools/sfx.py`, `thumbnail/cover.html` before you start. Your folder is a copy of it; keep its tech, replace the content.

## Brand rules (from the client brief, non-negotiable)
- Spelling: **MasterPajak AI**. Official tagline: **"Jualan jalan terus, pajaknya AI yang urus."** CTA: **"Coba kalkulator gratis"** + **"Link di bio"**.
- No DJP logos, stamps, Garuda, or any government/outlet branding.
- Any demo number on screen gets a small **"Ilustrasi data"** label. UI flows we have no screen recording of get a small **"Simulasi alur"** label.
- No tax rates, deadlines, penalties or guarantees on screen or in the script (no "0,5%", no "pasti hemat", no dates of obligations).
- Features may only be named as: pembukuan yang bisa ditelusuri, laporan laba rugi, rekonsiliasi bulanan/tahunan, unggah foto nota via aplikasi, estimasi pajak, asisten AI, tim ahli (always "sesuai layanan"), kalkulator gratis.
- The 3D robot host (our GLB, rendered live by `vendor/robot_mp.js` through `robot_perf.js`) appears in EVERY video, with its chest "M" hologram logo reveal at the brand moment and on the end card (hologram on screen ≥ 4 s, inside the safe zone, never touching frame edges).

## Look (series consistency)
- Palette (CSS vars in the copy): navy `#212C5F`, royal `#26367A`, deep `#131B40`, yellow `#FED111`, stabilo `#FFE440`, cloud `#F6F7FB`, text `#161D3A`, muted `#5B6478`.
- Fonts available in `assets/fonts`: Rubik 500/700/800 (display), Public Sans 400/600/700 (UI/body), Playfair Display 400/700/900 + italics (editorial accents), Plus Jakarta Sans 700/800, Poppins 400/700, Inter 400.
- The client asked for depth — NOT flat, front-facing 2D slides. Use CSS 3D perspective (tilted cards/pages/phones), layered parallax, real shadows, depth-of-field blur (see Video 1's 3D news rig: `.stage/.foc/.pan` + `#dofA/#dofB` backdrop-filter overlays, built inside `buildReady["news3d"]`), camera push-ins, whip transitions. Motion must be smooth (no jumps, no pops unless intentional), premium, not "8-bit".
- Light scenes (paper/cloud) for the "problem", navy scenes for the brand/solution, same as Video 1. Film grain overlay stays.
- **No text may overflow its box or bleed past its card/page edge** (the client called this "tembus" and rejected it). Every line of text must sit fully inside its container; on flat scenes no text may be cut by the frame edge either. Long words: size the text to fit, never rely on overflow.
- Safe zone: key text and the hologram inside x 60–1020, y 220–1600 (IG UI covers the bottom).

## Tech (HyperFrames 0.8.186, keep exactly as in the copy)
- One `index.html`; `#main-root` with `data-duration`; GSAP timeline `tl` paused and registered in `window.__timelines["main"]`; helpers `F(target, from, to, at)` (fromTo, `immediateRender:false`), `show/hide` (autoAlpha sets). Keep the backward-seek patch on `tl.render`, the `tl.eventCallback("onUpdate", … ROBOT_APPLY …)` line and the `tl.set({}, {}, TM.total - 0.01)` end marker.
- Animate ONLY with timeline tweens/sets (the renderer seeks; per-frame onUpdate code other than the robot line does not run). Anything that needs font metrics: compute inside a `window.__hf.buildReady["name"] = document.fonts.ready.then(…)` promise (tweens may be added to `tl` there).
- **All beat times live in `window.TM`** at the top of `index.html` (`key: number` pairs only — `tools/sfx.py` parses them by regex). Name a key for every VO line start so the film can be re-timed to the real VO later. Set `TM.total` and `data-duration` equal.
- Robot: `robot_perf.js` (keyframe channels → hold-then-move → spring filter). Use `pose(t, {...}, ease)`, `armL/armR(t, raise, fwd, bend, bendYaw, ease)`, `key(ch, t, v, ease)`. Position: `x` = horizontal centre, `y` = foot line, `h` = height in px; `y: 2700` = hidden below frame; half-body bottom = `y: 1660, h: 900–980`. Hologram channels `holoA` (alpha), `holoRev` (reveal 0→1.1 over ~1.2 s), `holoGl` (glitch), `hx, hy, hw` (centre x/y and width, keep inside 120 px side margins). Add every time the robot jumps (cut) to `CUTS`. Keep `NAVY` lighting on navy scenes. The robot may host earlier than Video 1 did (Video 1 hid it until the brand reveal by client request); it must not cover key text.
- Lip-sync: `lipsync.js` is loaded with `onerror` (the VO is not recorded yet, the voice is generated later with ElevenLabs). Plan beats for a VO pace of ~2.4 words/s plus short pauses; the robot's mouth will be driven later.
- SFX: adapt `tools/sfx.py` (synthesised whooshes, pops, ticks, marker, hologram fx, chime) to your beats; run `python3 tools/sfx.py` → `assets/audio/sfx.wav`.
- Thumbnail: `thumbnail/cover.html` (1080×1920, everything important inside the 4:5 crop y 285–1635, robot rendered live like Video 1's cover) → PNG `thumbnail/COVER_MP<NN>_<NAME>.png` via
  `node /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-testing-hyperframes/a8ae6177-e310-5942-a663-2ca4db15bda7/scratchpad/ml/cover.cjs <port> thumbnail/cover.html <abs out png>` (run with `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g)`).
