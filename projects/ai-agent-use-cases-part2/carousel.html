Use case 27 of 64

AI Avatar Generator.

Turn one photo or a text prompt into avatars and profile pictures.

Use it for

Team headshotsMatching professional headshots for the whole team. No photo shoot.
Game & stream avatarsAnime, cyberpunk or fantasy looks for Twitch, Discord and Steam.

How it works

  1. 1
    Upload a reference photo, or describe the style.
  2. 2
    Pick from several versions.Light, composition and style matched. Same person across the set.
  3. 3
    Download in high resolution.PNG or JPEG, sized for LinkedIn, Instagram, X or Discord.

Real example

A small agency of 8 swaps mismatched profile pics on its website and LinkedIn for one consistent headshot style, made from one selfie each.

AI Interior Design.

Redesign a room from a photo, stage an empty space, or turn a sketch into a real interior.

Use it for

Scandinavian refreshLight wood, white shelving, soft textiles.
Industrial officeDark steel, walnut, warm leather.

How it works

  1. 1
    Upload a clear room photo, then pick a style.
  2. 2
    Add specific asks.More storage, warmer colors, swap the layout or materials.
  3. 3
    Get a shopping list.Real furniture, cheaper swaps, and the first 3 changes to make.

Real example

A freelancer turns a bare spare room into a home office with shelving along one wall, then uses the image to brief his carpenter.

AI Math Solver.

Solves math step by step, with the reason behind each step.

Use it for

Typed3x − 7 = 14
Photo4(x + 2) = 20
Handwritten∫ 2x dx
PDFExam p.12

How it works

  1. 1
    Input the problem.
  2. 2
    It analyzes and plans.Names the concepts, picks the best method.
  3. 3
    It shows clear steps.Right formulas, matched to your level.
  4. 4
    It explains and checks.States each rule, verifies with a second method.
  5. 5
    Practice and more.3 practice problems, easy to hard, answers hidden.

AI Lead Management Tool.

Turn scattered leads into a scored, organized pipeline.

Use it for

SaaS teamsBANT scoring, routing rules, email templates and dashboards.
Real estate & dealersPriority rules, follow-up timing, call scripts and KPIs.

How it works

  1. 1
    Describe your business.Lead sources, sales steps, team size, customers, current problems.
  2. 2
    It researches proven systems.
  3. 3
    It builds your playbook.Scoring points, routing, nurture emails, follow-up timing.
  4. 4
    It maps fields to your CRM.Salesforce, HubSpot or Pipedrive.

Real example

A car dealership swaps a messy WhatsApp list for lead scoring. Salespeople call the hottest test-drive requests first.
