# reel-28 — techniques applied

White-theme remake of the "AI office with three employees" reference, moved to a **katering & event organizer**
business (a fresh vertical — the banned list is servis AC, laundry, toko, bengkel, dokter hewan, and the recently used
salon / barbershop / resto / klinik kecantikan).

Reference beats kept in order: you sleep → the office is all agents and the lights never go out → you used to do all
three roles alone → employee 1 (proposal) → employee 2 (produksi) → employee 3, the most important (analis) → the
winning package loops back → it runs 24/7 → the relief is time to think, not volume → "I don't work events, I run the
office". Dropped from the reference: its product names, its UI, and its "Komen MAU" DM CTA.

From `.claude/skills/aipreneur-reels-studio/references/technique_bank.md` + `mascot_robot_recipes.md`:

1. **Hook shows the problem, never states it** — a night→morning rail: 02.14 you are asleep, the dot travels, 06.30 the
   proposal is already written and stamped "Siap kirim". No slogan card, no badge.
2. **One object per scene, never repeated** — time rail → isometric cutaway office (three lit floors) → one avatar with
   three orbiting role tokens + 3/1/0 counters → quotation document that assembles from skeleton rows → rundown timeline
   with checklist and crew → funnel that filters five past events down to one package → triangle loop with a travelling
   package token → dashed 24/7 ring → big number that recedes behind a direction card → DULU/SEKARANG swap.
3. **A different transition on every beat** — zoom punch-through (hook→office), doors, zoom punch-in (token→app),
   3D card flip (proposal→produksi), stripes, zoom-out reveal (window→loop), rotate-morph (loop→ring), sweep,
   zoom-through (→CTA). No wipe type is used twice.
4. **Nothing appears from nothing** — every view is laid out with skeletons the moment it opens (quotation rows as grey
   pills, rundown slots as dashed tracks, checklist and crew at 45% opacity, criteria list greyed), then fills on the VO
   word. This is what keeps the window from ever sitting bare.
5. **Side rises stand beside, never on top** — the element is always on the opposite half and the background is dimmed:
   rise 1 `upR` with the "penawaran makin tajam" card on the left (loop dimmed to 26%), rise 2 `upL` with the direction
   card on the right. Hands alternate because `point` is side-aware: on the right the robot points with its left arm, on
   the left with its right arm.
6. **Camera stays at 1.0 inside the window.** Each view fills nearly the full 920 px frame, so any zoom would slice a
   panel and the no-cut guard would blank it. Life comes from element motion instead.

Colour: white theme only — `#294b77` is the accent, in-app data colours (blue/green/amber) stay inside the app, no red.
