# DESIGN SYSTEM — FREDDIE CLAUDE CODE 3D REMAKE (OVERRIDES OLD LIGHT HOUSE STYLE FOR THIS VIDEO)

## Style prompt

Cinematic digital editorial, a deep navy/blue gradient world anchored to Freddie's original supplied background, with a highly polished original 3D mascot performing intentional actions in tangible mini-studios, premium blue enamel/frosted panels, realistic soft contact shadows, moving camera parallax, physically connected match cuts, dramatic but restrained cyan word highlights, and clean sophisticated Poppins typography. The effect should feel crafted by a motion director with continuity and character acting, not a preset-heavy AI slideshow.

## Colors

| Name | Exact value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Supplied gradient | `assets/brand/background_original.png` | Entire persistent background; do not synthesize/recolor |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary titles, supporting captions, key light |
| Active Cyan | `#00D0FE` | Selected words, cursor/brackets, micro energy/glow and CTA |
| Panel Deep | `#0A0D2C` | Gradient start for captions/UI |
| Panel Blue | `#294B77` | Gradient end for captions/UI |

No red/purple as an extra brand color, no rainbow glow or hostile full-screen neon wash. Minor metallic grey neutrals may occur on 3D devices or suit assets as authentic material, not on text.

## Typography

- Poppins Bold 700: hero labels, six numbered steps, 1–3 highlighted words per phrase.
- Poppins Regular 400: sublabels, UI, brief explanatory annotations.
- Keep actual glyph outlines crisp, never motion-blur critical text into unreadability.

## Motion signature

- Camera on 3D sets: dolly 5–12%, subtle orbit max ~12°, differentiated parallax, focal-length plausibility.
- Props have material thickness, soft upper-left key, consistent rim/fill and shadow anchoring.
- Entrances arrive with meaningful world logic, often by a previous scene's physical object or geometry.
- Dramatic accents should resolve into a readable held composition; no repetitive bouncing.
- Existing mascot's face/arms/suit move with anticipation, follow-through, and purposeful gaze.
- Genuine logo fixed in upper-left, with periodic masked light sweep and rare 3% pulse.

## Don't

- Flat text-only slides, generic UI card grid for every shot, random zoom in/zoom out spam, repeated fades, shaking on every beat.
- Physically impossible character gestures, frozen mannequin arms, moonwalk feet, deformed jacket/strange lapels, fake generated character replacements.
- Overexposed bloom, cyan halo everywhere, glowing body outline, stretched background or stretched logo.
- Copying other creator's mascot, social handle, screenshots, CTA identity, original voice or music into finished work.
