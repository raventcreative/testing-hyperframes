# reel-32 — travel & tour operator, 10 AI agents (blue)

Reference: `1cca2a25-10_AI_Agent_for_Real_Estate.mp4` (43 s). Its structure is kept — hook naming "10 agents every
business needs" + "number 8 is my favourite", a rapid roster of all ten, then "they work 24/7 without an extra
employee", then CTA — but the vertical is moved from property to a travel & tour operator (reel-27 keeps property).

## Elements, one shape per scene (never the same twice)
1. **Hook** — a 2 x 5 board of the ten jobs filling up over one worker bar ("Lu sendiri") with a waiting counter.
   Shows the problem instead of stating it: no slogan card, no red badge.
2. **Roster** — the same 2 x 5 grid, now inside the app with numbers, colours and an "aktif" count. The hook board
   and the roster share their geometry, so the punch-through reads as a match cut.
3. **01 chat** — speech bubbles. **02 follow-up** — a horizontal 3-step track with a filling line.
   **03 booking** — a date row plus a 16-seat meter. **04 itinerary** — a 4-day strip joined by a line.
   **05 vendor** — three confirm plates. **06 konten** — a pop-out card beside the robot.
   **07 harga** — a three-box equation. **08 invoice** — an invoice card. **09 review** — a star card.
   **10 laporan** — a bar chart with two stat tiles.
4. **Favourite (08)** — the invoice leaves the window as a perforated ticket; the DP bar runs to LUNAS.
5. **24/7** — a conic dial counting to 24 beside two plates (1 orang / 10 agent) and ten agent chips.

## Transitions — a different one on every beat
zoom punch-through (hook to app) · slats · in-window cross-fades · blocks · iris · pop-out + dim ·
zoom punch-in (into the invoice) · zoom-out reveal (window shrinks away, dial lands) · zoom-out recede to the CTA.
No wipe type is used twice.

## From the technique bank
- **Hook shows the problem, never states it** (ten jobs land on one person).
- **Shared element / match cut**: the hook's job board becomes the agent roster.
- **Camera never still**: one eased move per scene, no zoom-in then zoom-out back to back.
- **Cross-fade, never a blank window**: the outgoing shape only leaves once the new one is in.

## Client rules checked
- **Hands alternate**: 20 gestures, strictly R/L/R/L… verified by replaying `side()` against the pose track.
- **Robot**: never centred over the element being explained; two side rises (upR beside the konten card on the left,
  upL beside the invoice card on the right); no low poses, never zoomed; whole and centred at the CTA.
- **VO**: tags kept in a narrow band; every pause already <= 0.42 s, so no padding was added.
- **Length**: 53.4 s (target 45–60 s).
